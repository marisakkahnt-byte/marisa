BULLETIN BOARD — "story & stitches" (behind the sewing desk)

THE LOOK
• Cover the cork in cream linen (or ticking stripe), then do a French memo-board lattice
  with blush grosgrain ribbon (1/2"), tacked with gold upholstery nails where ribbons cross.
  Tuck cards behind the ribbons, so it looks styled even when nothing is pinned.
• Gold frame: paint the existing frame with gold leaf paint, or add a gold gimp trim.
• Gold ball push pins only (one color keeps it chic).

THREE ZONES (left to right)
1. I DO · the words (cream / espresso / blush / gold)
   - I Do logo seal sticker, "Story is the signature," quill seal
   - "this week" card: scripts, rehearsals, vow calls, thank-yous
   - a favorite client note (printed, no last names)
   - one favorite couple photo (photographer credited on the back), swap seasonally
2. YOU · the girl behind both (center, gold)
   - your zebra-top headshot as a polaroid
   - rainy days, a little luck (clover), iced matcha / Muji pens / the Longchamp
   - a "story & stitches" card tying the two brands together
3. STITCHES · the hands (emerald / blush / zebra / cheetah)
   - a REAL mini needlepoint you stitch (e.g. "certified diva"), the star of the board
   - prize-ribbon rosettes ("she's a gem," "certified diva")
   - keepsake cards (zebra "keep the good stuff," rhinestone "she's a gem")
   - thread palette card with real thread snips taped on

PRINT LIST (all in Drive)
• Stickers folder: I Do round 2 #1, #2, #3, #10 · Stitches wordmark
• Keepsake cards: card-03 zebra, card-07 rhinestone (print 4x6)
• Diva drop: certified-diva needlepoint (as a pattern to stitch), rosette stickers (print ~4")
• Middle cards (rainy days, a little luck, story & stitches, little loves): ask and I'll export print files

TIP: keep about 20% of the board empty so it breathes; rotate seasonal pieces.
