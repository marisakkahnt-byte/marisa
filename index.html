How I work

  • CuriousFirst question is always "why do we do it this way?" Second is usually "can we not?"
  • Born with the geneThe "how hard can it be" gene, specifically. If I don't know how to do something yet, that's just Tuesday. I ramp fast and figure it out.
  • Leads with empathyPeople are usually doing their best with a broken process. I assume good intent first, always.
  • "Yes, and"I treat work like improv: always looking for the "yes, and," not the reason it won't work. Failing just means I learned something before you had to.
  • Allergic to extra stepsIf a process has more than five steps, I'm already redesigning it in my head. I promise I'm listening too.
  • Knows the roomI can tell you exactly who needs to be looped in, who doesn't, and who just wants the recap after.
  • Doesn't gatekeepI'd rather teach you the fix than be the only one who can do it. When one of us shines, we all shine.
  • CraftI sweat the details nobody asked for. The last 10% is the part people remember.
