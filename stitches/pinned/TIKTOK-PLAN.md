# TikTok plan: I Do by Marisa + Marisa Stitches

## How to win on TikTok (the short version)
- **Hook in the first second.** Put text on screen right away. The first line has to make someone stop scrolling.
- **7–15 seconds** is the sweet spot. Shorter beats longer.
- **Add a trending sound inside the TikTok app.** Tap "Add sound" and pick one with the 📈 arrow, set low under your video. Your own voiceover works great too.
- **Post 3x a week** per account. Consistent beats perfect.
- **Reuse everything:** each TikTok can also go up as an Instagram Reel, and vice versa.
- **Reply to comments with videos.** It's the easiest next post there is.
