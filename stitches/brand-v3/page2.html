12 · diva rules

  • Attitude in the words, chic in the look: emerald, blush, gold, crystal and pearl. Pink can be glossier here, but no rainbow.
  • Doll-world references are a feeling only. Never use real toy names, logos or characters.
  • Rhinestone script = our gems + pearls, spelled stone by stone.
  • Humor is relatable maker life. Use her real stitching photos, never movie or TV stills.
  • Inspo (Kristin Jones tees, Pinterest saves) is for vibe only. Always our own phrases and art.
