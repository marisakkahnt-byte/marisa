08 · rules

  • Clean base first. Oat, linen, ink and emerald do most of the work.
  • More sparkle, still chic. Real-looking cut gems and crystal stars. Up to ~6 on a graphic, confetti allowed on special posts. Never cartoon gems.
  • Heirloom, but cool. Gold thread, emerald and cream, mixed with classic cheetah and zebra. Grandma’s linens meet a downtown girl.
  • Prints, not rainbow. Classic cheetah, black-and-white zebra, emerald or ink polka dot. 2–3 colors max, about 1 in 5 posts, usually behind a clean cream card.
  • Pink is a whisper. Blush is the only pastel now, a soft pop.
  • Grid rhythm. Alternate light / dark / print / your photo so no row is all one color.
  • Your photos win. Real stitching photos, shown whole. Gems go around them, never on top of the work.
  • For stitching: logo works in one thread color; the gem dots become two French knots or tiny satin dots.
