FAQ

faq.txt
How do I place an order?
Only through the order form on this site.
How do I reach you?
Orders: the form. Questions: this FAQ. Anything else: DM me on Instagram.
What can you embroider?
Almost anything that fits in a hoop: hats, sweatshirts, totes, towels, baby things, napkins.
Do you supply the item?
You do! Drop it off or ship it to me. I also have canvas pouches, bibs & bath towels for $5 each.
How much does it cost?
$15 per spot for text & monograms, $20 for designs. The form shows a live estimate.
How do I pay?
Cash App, Venmo, cash, or check, paid in full before pickup or shipping.
Do you ship?
Yes! $7 flat anywhere in the US, or free porch pickup in Clinton Twp.
What's your return policy?
All sales are final. If I make a mistake, I'll fix it, so check your mockup carefully!
I don't know what font or color I want.
No problem. I have 100+ fonts. Tap your faves in Fonts & Colors and I'll send options.
