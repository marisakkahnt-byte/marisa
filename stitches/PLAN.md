## What the brand is today
- **What:** custom machine embroidery, stitched on a Brother machine in Clinton Township, MI, and shipped everywhere
- **Product lines:**
  - Monograms and names
  - Baby gifts
  - Towels, napkins and pouches (cocktail napkins included)
  - Shirts and hats
- **Voice:** "A serial hobbyist who took it too far." "Charm, threaded in." "If it holds still long enough, I'll stitch it." Warm, funny, a little cheeky.
- **Look:**
  - Colors: cream `#f1e6cc`, forest green `#2f4a3a`, gold `#c9a24a`, navy `#1f2a44` and pink `#f2a7bb`
  - Pattern: leopard spots
  - Mascot: a sleepy bulldog

## The big idea: Stitches makes the I Do merch
The "By the power vested in me" hat and the "I marry people." tote don't need Printify if you stitch them yourself.
- **Margin:** about $5–8 in blanks, versus about $18–31 a hat through Printify.
- **Quality:** you check every piece before it ships.
- **Personalizing:** names and dates can be added inside the embroidery, which Printify can't do.
- **Both brands win:** I Do sells the story, and Stitches makes the thing.

Keep Printify for what you can't stitch, like the engraved pen and printed mugs.

## Everyday product ideas
- **Name or word on the sleeve or collar** of a crewneck: small, tonal, quiet luxury
- **Tiny icon hats**: one small stitched symbol (a bow, a martini, a dog face) on a washed cap
- **Pet ear-outline embroidery** from a customer's photo: the outline only, the pet's name underneath
- **Baby name sweaters and swaddle blankets**: expands your baby-gift line
- **"Club" crewnecks**, in your own wording: "Book Club," "Dog Mom Club" and so on, in your fonts and colors
- **Cocktail napkins with funny one-liners**, sold in sets of 4 or 6 as hostess gifts

*Note: TikTok trends move fast. Use your own wording and designs, never another maker's exact design.*

## TikTok and Reels content (formats that suit embroidery)
1. **Satisfying close-up:** the needle stitching a name, 10–15 seconds. No talking needed.
2. **"Stitching your name"** series: commenters drop names, and you stitch them.
3. **Hoop reveal:** the hoop comes off, then the finished piece. End on the reveal.
4. **Pack an order with me:** tissue, sticker, a handwritten note, a dog cameo.
5. **Hobbyist-who-took-it-too-far** storytime, in your voice.
6. **Crossover posts:** "My other job is marrying people…", then the officiant hat being stitched. Each brand sends people to the other.

## Ops that make it efficient
- **One order form:**
  - Item, color, thread color, text, font (3 choices max) and deadline
  - Upload space for an icon or pet photo
- **Three fonts only:** script, serif and block. Fewer choices means faster decisions.
- **Turnaround promise:** for example, "ships in 7–10 business days." Set this to whatever you can really keep.
- **Proof first, then stitch** for anything custom (a quick mockup image)
- **Pricing tiers:**
  - Base item
  - Plus up to 10 characters
  - Plus a second location (sleeve or back)
  - Set discounts for wedding party orders
