# Marisa Stitches: growth plan

*Working doc, based on marisastitches.com (read Nov 25, 2026). Only one TikTok has been shared with Claude so far, so the inspo-based ideas will get sharper once the others come in.*

## What the brand is today (from the site)
- **Studio:** custom machine embroidery on one Brother machine, in Clinton Township, MI. Ships anywhere in the US.
- **Model:** all custom, and you bring the item. A small stock of canvas pouches, bibs and bath towels sells for $5 each.
- **Pricing:**
  - $15 per placement for names, text and monograms
  - $20 per placement for designs and detailed fills
  - $7 flat shipping, or free porch pickup in Clinton Twp
  - Rush: +$5, or +$10 for bigger orders
- **Process:**
  - Order form with a live estimate
  - Reply within 48 hours with a quote
  - The customer gets the item to you
  - Digital mockup proof
  - Stitch
  - Paid in full before pickup or shipping (Cash App, Venmo, cash or check)
  - All sales final, but you fix your own mistakes
- **Status:** the shop is currently closed ("taking a little break from orders"). Instagram is the place to follow along until it reopens.
- **Voice:** "A serial hobbyist who took it too far." "Charm, threaded in." Tech girl by day, which is why there's "a very organized form."
- **Look:**
  - Colors: oat `#F4EFE7`, forest green `#1F4D3A`, navy `#1E2F4A`, gold `#C89A3C`, pink `#d6336c`
  - Fonts: Playfair Display, plus playful "file" labels like `order_form.txt` and `fonts.exe`
- **Proof it works:** the reviews are full of 😭💗, "I love u for life," and "Baxter" (a dog!).

**What this tells us:** your system is already efficient, and it's built so the business can stay small. Any growth should keep it that way: few new things, each one easy to repeat.

## The big idea: Stitches makes the I Do merch
The "By the power vested in me" hat and the "I marry people." tote don't need Printify. Stitch them yourself.
- **The math:** a blank hat costs about $6–9 at wholesale. With your $15 placement fee, that's roughly $21–24 of cost and labor, and it sells at about $34. Printify's embroidered hat alone costs about $18–31.
- **Personalizing:** add the couple's names and wedding date as a second placement, for +$15. Printify can't do that.
- **You check every piece** before it ships.
- **Keep Printify only** for things you can't stitch: the engraved pen and printed mugs.
- **This is the one exception** to "you bring the item": you'd keep a small stock of blank hats and totes, like you already do with pouches, bibs and towels.
