07 · Rules that keep it chic (not chaotic)

  • One gem color + white per post. Save the full rainbow and the holographic gradient for logos, stickers and big announcements.
  • Sparkles ✦ in threes, max. Diamonds are the signature shape. No unicorns, dolphins or cartoon animals (that's someone else's brand); the sleepy bulldog can stay as yours.
  • Keep the voice: "serial hobbyist who took it too far" now just has more glitter.
  • Embroidery-friendly: the logo works in 1–3 thread colors, so it can be stitched on your own hats and totes.
