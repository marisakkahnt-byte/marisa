07 · Rules

  • Neutral first. Oat, linen and white do 90% of the work. Gems are little accents, like jewelry on an outfit.
  • No rainbow gradients. Gems keep their own color; 1–3 per graphic, up to ~10 scattered in a reel.
  • Type is lowercase, big and tight. Keep the voice: "big hobby girl over here."
  • For stitching: the logo works in one thread color (ink or a single gem color).
