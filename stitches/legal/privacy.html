privacy policy

Last updated [DATE]

Marisa Stitches Studio is a one-person custom embroidery studio in Clinton Township, Michigan. This page explains what I collect when you place an order, why, and the choices you have. Short version: I only use your information to make and deliver your order, and I never sell it.

What I collect

  • From the order form: your name, phone number, email, how you'd like your mockup sent, the item details, exact wording, font and color picks, placement, inspo photos and links, your deadline and any notes.
  • If your order ships: your mailing address.
  • Payment: I accept Cash App, Venmo, cash and check. Those apps process the payment under their own privacy policies. I don't see or store your card or bank details.
  • Messages: anything you send me by text, email or Instagram DM about your order.

How I use it

  • To quote, design, stitch and deliver your order
  • To send your mockup and ask questions about your order
  • To keep simple records of past orders, so I can fix a mistake or redo an order you loved
  • To post a photo of your finished item, only if you checked the permission box

I don't send marketing emails unless you've separately signed up for them, and I never sell or rent your information.

Who I share it with

Only the services that help me run the studio: my website and form host, USPS (your address, for shipping), and the payment app you choose. They only receive what they need to do that job.

Photos and social media

Inspo photos you upload are used only for your order. I'll only post a photo of your finished item on @marisastitches if you opted in, and I'll wait until it's been received or gifted. You can ask me to take a post down any time, just send me a message.
