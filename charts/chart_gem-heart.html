BEFORE YOU START

how to use this chart

  • Each square on the chart = one tent stitch on your canvas. This is a needlepoint chart: work every square, background included, in tent stitch (continental or basketweave).
  • Canvas: 13-mesh mono or interlock canvas is easiest (4.6" × 4.2" design). For a smaller, finer piece use 18 mesh (3.3" × 3.1"). Leave at least 2" of bare canvas on every side for finishing.
  • Thread: match the colors in the key with any brand you love (wool, cotton, silk or blends). On 13 mesh use thread that covers the canvas well; on 18 mesh go thinner.
  • Start by finding the center (the bold lines mark every 10 stitches) and work the design first, then fill the background.
  • Finish it as a pillow, a pouch insert, an ornament, a framed piece or a key fob, whatever you like.
