$36K is the
new normal

Zola’s 2026 First Look Report (11,500+ couples) puts the average wedding at $36,000 with 145 guests, holding steady for a second year.

The lessonIf you’re trimming, trim the extras. The ceremony is the part that actually makes it a wedding, and the part everyone replays.

the timeline

02

Weddings are
turning into weekends

The Knot reports 71% of wedding celebrations now span two to three days, with multiple events.

The lessonMore events means more speeches. Keep your ceremony the one quiet, focused 20 minutes where everyone is in the same room, paying attention.

Source: The Knot, Real Weddings Study
