About Marisa

Hi, I’m Marisa.

This work started with a simple ask: officiate a friend’s wedding. What I didn’t expect was how much I would care about getting it right — telling their story in a way that felt true, thoughtful, and completely their own. That’s become the foundation of everything I do.

A ceremony isn’t a formality. It’s the most personal story you’ll ever tell.

Written with care. Designed to sound like you.

I’m drawn to the in-between moments — the way you speak to each other, the shared humor, the quiet details that make you you.

The Process

How it works.

  1. I

    We commit

    Contract, deposit, date held. Then I ask one simple question: what’s your vibe? Hands-off or involved at every step — both are welcome.

  2. II

    I learn your story

    I want to know your story well enough to tell it. A Google Form, phone call, or voice notes give the most honest, personal reflection.

  3. III

    I write it

    I take the pieces of your story, add my craft, and write a ceremony that could only ever be yours. Then I send it for review — or not.

  4. IV

    We refine

    Rounds of edits and tone tweaks — or opt out and let me refine it solo. Completely your call.
