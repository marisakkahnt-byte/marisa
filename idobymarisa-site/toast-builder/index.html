Marisa's rules for a great toast
  • Introduce yourself first. Half the room doesn't know you.
  • Talk about both of them, not just your person.
  • No exes, no secrets, no inside jokes the room won't get.
  • Two to four minutes. Shorter is always better.
  • Have one drink before, not three. Bring cards, not your phone.
  • End with the glass up and their names.
